Generative AI and Cognitive Surrender

“Why do things if AI can do them for you?”

So, what is cognitive surrender? Simply put, it’s when you forgo all agency to let something else, like AI, do everything for you. Write an email to your boss? AI can do that. Research symptoms of an illness? AI can do that. Think critically and form your own opinions and judgement? Why would you when AI can do it for you? It gets to a point where you forget how to think and feel for yourself.

This has become a big problem for a lot of people, especially those who rely heavily on AI. They give up on trying to do anything to just let artificial intelligence do it for them. They stop using their critical thinking skills and they stop second guessing information that they come across on the internet. Having media literacy is very important, both for staying safe and informed, and for having a healthy brain.
