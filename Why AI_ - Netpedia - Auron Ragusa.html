Gen AI’s issues

AI has given so many good things in the past so why is gen AI so different? Well, firstly in order to feed it, it needs a constant stream of good images, articles, videos, basically whatever they can get their hands on, copyrighted or not. This poses the first major issue, it takes art and reference from EVERYONE, copyrighted films have been and are being fed to AI, Creative Commons is completely ignored along with it so along with creating low effort posts for whoever uses it, it also discourages a lot of artists because they know that all their creations will be fed into the AI blender. AI needs a constant stream of media. This is an issue with Copyright due to taking any media they can find. This discourages artists and ignores CC agreements. While AI made media is not able to be copyrighted, AI using copyrighted media is still controversial.

Another big issue with AI that a large community of people have complained about, the computer components market. AI has been buying up and using HUGE amounts of components, RAM being the biggest requirement followed closely by GPU demand. This not only makes people pay more for GPU and RAM but also makes them pay more for silicon chips as a whole. Things like Raspberry PIs and Arduinos have been hiking in price due to their components costing more and more, specifically VRAM. Another reason that these components are becoming more expensive is because these parts companies are selling these components in bulk to AI foundations. In addition to this most AI companies aren't even making much money to fund this the main cycle follows AI companies buying components, and parts companies using the money they made from selling to AI to buy more use of AI.

AI exhausts large amounts of RAM, VRAM, GPUs, and many other computer components. This mass purchasing of these products raised the cost of silicon chips in the Consumer market. AI buys components, Component companies buy AI.

AI is known to be bad for the environment, but most people don't know the extent of it, the majority of the issues are pinned to the water AI datacenters out off into the atmosphere, water vapor being a greenhouse gas. But when does AI actually become bad for the environment you may ask? EVERY STEP OF THE WAY. Firstly, the silicon mining to get these computer parts causes large amounts of pollution by polluting water and ground as well as needing a lot of power. Secondly when the silicon is being manufactured it makes pollutants via the byproducts that go mostly unused. The construction of the datacenters take up land and need a huge amount of power to keep all those components working. Most of the time this power comes from nuclear plants, which make nuclear byproducts and even more steam. And lastly running the datacenters also use a lot of water which comes out as steam.

AI causes more ground and water pollution due to silicon mining, creates a greater need for power which is often made in unsustainable ways, and emits large amounts of steam into the air.

What is AI?

Alright, now that I’m done pointing out AI at its issues, what actually is AI? I mentioned reinforcement learning earlier, what that? Well AI in reality is a huge net of nodes and axons to connect those nodes, each axon will multiply or divide the value fed into it and goes into outputs at the end, be it Images, Text, or sound (example image to right). While in A proper AI agent’s case the neural net will be much larger having maybe hundreds of layers and tens of thousands of nodes, the specific layout in the image is an example of a deep learning network however there are many other layouts.

AI is a large neural net in reality with many layers of nodes and axons to connect those nodes. This net takes in information and then makes an output with it. The example is a small version and real neural networks of proper agents are much larger.

Alright so the structure of an AI is cool and all but how do we turn it into a client? And why does doing that take so much processing power? Well first of all once you have your neural net you need to pick your clients environment and pick out your clients goal. Then you have to give it input and output. So for the sake of being easy to understand we will make a client that wants to jump as high as possible. We will make the task getting as high as possible, and make the input the current state of all its joints. Then we will make its output the ability to move its joints. Now that we have our basics we run a simulation lets say 100 times. And with this new population we will give them all a neural net but change them by random across the population. After the 100 simulations we will rank the 100 clients from worst to best, pick a few of the highest performers then remove all the others, in the empty slots we will copy the best clients with a brain that is slightly altered at random. Then we repeat this process many many many times. After all is said and done, hopefully we will have a client that is able to jump decently well. Now we have a decent jumping bot, but only in one environment so lets make the topography of its floor random, with this it will soon start to learn how to jump even from this, and how about now trying to get it to move in a direction and as we keep adding things its able to do we start to go in the direction of what ChatGPT and other companies are trying to achieve, Artificial General Intelligence.

Reinforcement learning is one of the main ways of training an agent. It involves taking a starting population and iterating on it, picking only the fittest to continue to the next generation of iteration.
