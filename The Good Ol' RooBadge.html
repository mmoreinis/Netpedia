Roobadge

Why it is the most stupid yet innovative thing ever imagined by Australia

The magnificent Roobadge is pretty much a speaker you put on your car to keep kangaroos from totaling it in head on collisions by using, and I'm not making this up, region specific noises of predators that eat kangaroos to scare it off!

Reasons why this abomination of a Speaker and GPS was born

Kangaroos are annoying buggers. They are 6 feet tall and 900 pounds of pure muscle, and have a very nasty habit of going into roads and picking fights with any And once you stop because you don't want to kill the marsupial icon, they will beat the absolute brakes of your vehicle, and even if you do hit it, not only have you now killed the mascot of Australia and your rusty chelvolt is now scrap metal. So in order to prevent this catastrophe from happening to your precious automobile(Oh and I guess to stop killing the kangaroos as well) they have created the Roobadge to prevent this from happening.

How this bloody thing works.

The Roobadge works using a Bluetooth connected to the GPS inside of the care that, when out in the wilderness, it will turn on and see which area there in, take a deep breath in, and make a noise based on which predators hunt that specific species of kangaroo in that specific region of Australia wilderness. Honestly its really cool in my opinion especially since it shuts off in cities and turns on in the wilderness.
