Social Media Algorithms: Friend or Foe?

What social media algorithms are and their power over users.

Did you know social media algorithms can control what information you see?


What are social media algorithms?

Social media algorithms are the algorithms (a step-by step set of instructions) that determine what kind of content you see on social media. They use data, such as what posts are most popular, what you give the most attention to (likes, comments, shares), and what similar users enjoy to choose what content is most relevant to you and prioritize showing you that content over others.

What is the purpose of social media algorithms?

The main point of social media algorithms is to keep you engaged. They provide you hyper-personalized content which may influence you to stay on the platform longer, in turn making a company more money. Some people criticize these algorithms for their contribution to the spread of extremist content, misinformation, and their addictive qualities.

Highly controversial or outrage-inspiring posts tend to get more engagement, so social media algorithms push them further than other posts. Along with this, as you interact more with certain things, the algorithm reinforces what you pick, possibly leading to increasingly polarizing and intense content. This kind of content is more likely to include misinformation.

For example:

TikTok, a popular social media platform for sharing short-form content, uses an algorithm. It has a For You Page which displays recommended content based on how you have interacted with posts previously, constantly evolving the content it shows you to better suit your interests and what you will interact most with.

Overall:

Social media algorithms can be a helpful tool when wanting to see content interesting to you, but can also create echo chambers and spread misleading information. They may result in us spending more time than we intended to on a platform as well. It’s important to stay aware of how social media shapes our perception and the way we interact with the internet so we can notice when these things are happening.
