Neuralink

Do you want to text people with your mind?

Neuralink is a chip that is implanted into your brain so you can text people with your thoughts

This is an example of the chip that would be surgically implanted into your brain. With the Neuralink you can text and call people with your thoughts instead of having to pick up your phone. The first Neuralink is called "Telapathy". It is designed to enable paralyzed patients to control devices by thoughts.

Are implants good?

Some people support Neuralink while others don't. The people who support say it is good for the disabled people who can't communicate as easily as others, on the other hand, people who don't support are very skeptical about the long term effect the Neuralink could have on you. Another concern is the amount of animal testing that the company has done to perfect the product. At least 1,500 animals were used for animal testing. Many of them were euthanized after testing. They are still testing the safety of the implant and the robot.

This company has had multiple flaws over the years

In 2023 DoT investigators found the company had failed to register itself as a transporter of hazardous material. They also found improperly packaged hazardous waste including, Xylene, a flammable liquid that can cause dizziness, confusion, loss of muscle coordination, headaches, and death.
