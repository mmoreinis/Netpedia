The First Computer Virus

Computer Viruses:I AM THE CREEPER

The Creeper was the first computer virus to be created. It didn’t damage any data or software, it simply flashed the words “I’M THE CREEPER : CATCH ME IF YOU CAN!” across the screen. It only affected some scientist computer, who at the time didn't know what had happened and brushed it off. Creeper was a test by a scientist but the idea of a virus had been around fro a little while before Creeper was made.

The image shown above is from a simulation of the coded virus Creeper, whose intent was to flash this across the person screen to see if it could replicate itself and go across different screens.

Even a small computer virus can take a lot of code to make.

The Reaper

Reaper was the first anti-virus created. It chased after Creeper and was able to stop it from spreading more. It was the first of its kind. It was made by the same people who made Creeper. I did the same thing as the Creeper but instead of flashing words across the screen it went after the Creeper and stopped it from flashing the words across the screen.
