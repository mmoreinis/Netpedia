Is AI Taking Over the Music Industry?

What has AI already done the the music Industry?

Spotify has been using AI to help people filter through music they like and may not like for their playlists and before anybody knew it their playlist had been full of songs that they never directly put in.

Music AI, what is it and why do people dislike it?

“I was enjoying my regular evening walk with my earphones and Spotify blasting into my head. Two songs by a certain artist, Room, came on. I liked them, didn’t think much of it, added them to the playlist and moved on. “ Alwyn Lau

People Don't like not making their choices and having someone else do it for them, in this case, AI choosing what song you listen to.

But, is the AI Useful?

Personally, I love AI in this situation.
