What are Internet Routers?

Did you ever wonder how each of your computers in your house gets different information from the internet?

Describing an internet router

The job of a router is to connect one network to another,a router can create A smaller network comprised of these devices so that they can share data among each other

An internet router manages the local network traffic, it sends bits and bytes between the connected computers. A router's primary objective is to route data between devices at home. A router is not technically necessary but most of the time it is necessary. Yes you are able to use a modem but a modem is only able to connect to one single device, with an internet router you can access the internet with multiple devices. So basically an internet router is something that routes the internet into multiple devices, without it, a modem could only provide internet to one device that is connected to it.


Examples

Things like tour phones, printers, tablets, TVs, and smart security systems all require's something to connect them onto the internet, and that is where a router would be used.

Imagine one of your family members or a friend staying over at your home is streaming a film, another is working on a document on google, and someone is playing some games online. The routers assign the necessary bandwidth each device needs for whatever is being done.
